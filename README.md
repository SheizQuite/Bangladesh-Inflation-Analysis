# Bangladesh Inflation Rate Analysis (1987–2025)

## Project Description

A 39-year statistical analysis of Bangladesh's inflation dynamics from 1987 to 2025 looking at long-term trends, volatility across periods, and what it means for consumers and businesses. All analysis was carried out using the C programming language.

## Goal

The goal of this project is to understand how inflation in Bangladesh has changed over time, compare different periods, identify unusually high or low inflation years, and examine what these patterns can mean for consumers and businesses.

## Dataset

* Country: Bangladesh
* Period: 1987–2025
* Number of years: 39
* Data: Annual inflation rate
* Format: CSV

## Programming Language

- C

## What I Did


* Read the inflation data of Bangladesh from a CSV file using C.

* Extracted the year and the annual inflation rate.

* Calculated the year-to-year percentage-point changes in inflation.

* Identified the year that had the highest inflation rate.

* Identified the year that had the lowest inflation rate.

* Calculated the average inflation rate.

* Compared inflation across three periods:
  - 1987–1999
  - 2000–2012
  - 2013–2025

* Calculated standard deviation to compare inflation volatility across periods.

* Compared more recent inflation patterns.

* Examined reasons behind years that had unusually high or low inflation.

* Considered the implications of inflation, for consumers and businesses.

## Key Findings
- Highest inflation: **2011 — 11.40%**
- Lowest inflation: **2001 — 2.01%**
- Overall average inflation: **6.54%**

### Average Inflation by Period
| Period | Average Inflation |
|---|---:|
| 1987–1999 | 6.17% |
| 2000–2012 | 6.45% |
| 2013–2025 | 7.01% |

The 2013–2025 period had the highest average inflation among the three periods.

### Inflation Volatility

The period from 2000 to 2012 experienced the inflation volatility with a standard deviation of 2.65.

The period from 2013 to 2025 had a standard deviation of 1.68. This shows that even though the average inflation during this period was higher the yearly inflation rates stayed closer to that average.

### Year-to-Year Changes

The project also computes how many percentage points inflation rose or fell from one year to the next. This helps spot times when inflation changed quickly instead of only looking at the yearly inflation rate.

### Consumer and Business Implications

Shifting inflation can hurt consumers’ purchasing power and raise household expenses. For businesses inflation changes can alter costs, pricing decisions, planning and profitability.
